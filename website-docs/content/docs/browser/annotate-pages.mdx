---
title: Annotate a page for an agent
description: Click parts of a web page, write a note on each, and give the notes to an agent.
---

<Callout type="info">This works in the desktop app only.</Callout>

Telling an agent "the blue button in the sidebar is too small" leaves it guessing which code you mean. In a [browser tab](/docs/browser/browser-tabs) you can click the button itself and write your note on it. Each note carries the element's selector, so the agent can search the code for it. Notes on an area also carry a cropped screenshot.

## Start annotating

Open the page in a browser tab and click **Annotate page** (the pen) to the right of the address bar.

The address bar changes into the annotation toolbar. From left to right it has:

- **Done**, which ends annotation and brings the address bar back. Your notes stay.
- The tools: **Comment**, **Rectangle**, and **Ruler**.
- **Area** and **Visible**, which copy a screenshot to the clipboard.
- **Pause animations**, **Hide annotations & guides** (the eye), and **Copy feedback**.
- The number of notes, and **Send to chat**.

## Add notes to a page

Pick a tool, then use it on the page:

- **Comment** (`C`): click an element, such as a button or a heading.
- **Rectangle** (`R`): drag a box around a part of the page.
- **Ruler** (`M`): see [Mark alignment and spacing with the ruler](#mark-alignment-and-spacing-with-the-ruler).

A box opens next to what you picked. The part you picked stays outlined while the box is open. Type your note in it, then click **Add comment** or press `Ctrl+Enter` (`Cmd+Enter` on macOS). Press `Esc` or click **Cancel** to close the box without a note.

Each note gets a numbered pin on the page. Troupe saves a cropped screenshot of the part you marked with each note.

## Mark alignment and spacing with the ruler

With **Ruler** on, a dashed guide line follows your pointer, so you can see where a guide will go before you click. Its label shows the position, such as `V · x 240px`, and the distance to the nearest guide that runs the same way. Hold `Shift` to switch the line from vertical to horizontal.

- Click to place a red vertical guide line. `Shift`+click places a horizontal one. After you click in the page, you can also press `V` or `H` to add a vertical or horizontal guide.
- Each guide shows its position, such as `x 240px`. When a guide is a few pixels off from the edge of a nearby element, it also shows how far off it is, such as `+3px off`. Labels of guides that are close together don't cover each other.
- To move a guide, drag its line or the handle on it. The box for the guide hides while you drag and opens again 2 seconds after you let go.
- When you place a guide or click one, a box opens where you can describe the change. Click **Remove guide** to take the guide away. When there is another guide that runs the same way, **Save spacing** saves the distance between the two guides as a measurement instead.

Guides and measurements go into your notes like other notes.

## Take a screenshot

You can copy a screenshot of the page to the clipboard:

- **Area** (`Ctrl+Shift+A`, `Cmd+Shift+A` on macOS): drag a box around the part you want. Press `Esc` to stop without a screenshot.
- **Visible** (`Ctrl+Shift+S`, `Cmd+Shift+S` on macOS): take the part of the page you can see.

The **Screenshot** button (the camera) next to the address bar has the same two options, **Screenshot area** and **Screenshot visible**, so you don't have to start annotating first.

Troupe copies the image to the clipboard and shows **Screenshot copied to clipboard**. Screenshots don't go into your list of notes. To give one to an agent, paste it into the chat's message box with `Ctrl+V` (`Cmd+V` on macOS).

## Check your notes

Click the number next to **Send to chat** to see the notes you collected. Each one shows its number, your note, the element, and the screenshot if it has one.

- Click the trash button on a note to delete it.
- **Clear all** deletes every note.

## Give the notes to an agent

1. Click **Send to chat**. Troupe copies the notes to the clipboard and removes them from the list.
2. Open the chat with your agent.
3. Paste the notes into the message box with `Ctrl+V` (`Cmd+V` on macOS), add any instructions, and send.

The agent receives one line per note, with the element's selector or the area, and your comment. When Troupe saved a screenshot for a note, the line also has the path of the image file, so the agent can open it.

**Copy feedback** copies the same text but keeps the notes in the list.

Troupe also keeps a copy of your notes in a local database on your computer. Agents don't read it on their own, so always paste the notes into the chat.

## Other toolbar buttons

- **Pause animations** (`P`) freezes animations and videos, so you can mark an element that moves. Click **Paused** to start them again.
- **Hide annotations & guides** (the eye, `H`) hides the note pins, rectangle boxes, and ruler guides with their labels. Click **Show annotations & guides** to show them again.

The keys `C`, `R`, `M`, `P`, and `H` work when the keyboard focus is on Troupe's toolbar, not inside the page. If a key does nothing, click the button in the toolbar instead.
